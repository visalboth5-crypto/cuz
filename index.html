Clear answers to the questions people actually ask. Start anywhere.

01THC & CBD, explained+

THC and CBD are two of many cannabinoids in the cannabis plant. THC can produce intoxicating effects; CBD is not intoxicating. Products and individual responses vary, and neither percentage alone predicts how something will feel.

02Indica, sativa & hybrid+

These labels are commonly used to describe plant varieties, but they are not reliable guarantees of a particular effect. A product’s cannabinoid and terpene profile, dose, and your own body all matter.

03Terpenes & flavor notes+

Terpenes are aromatic compounds found in many plants, including cannabis. They contribute to aroma and flavor. Research into their effects is ongoing, so treat tasting notes as descriptions—not promises.

04Methods & mindful dosing+

Inhaled and ingested products can have very different onset times and durations. If you choose to consume, start with a low dose, wait before considering more, and follow the product label and local guidance.

05Safety, health & the law+

Cannabis is not for everyone. Avoid use if pregnant or breastfeeding, keep products locked away from children and pets, and never drive or operate machinery after use. Laws vary by location; check local rules and speak with a healthcare professional about health questions.
